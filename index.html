Why do I need to confirm in the wallet?

The scanner reads balances through the standard wallet bridge. One confirmation establishes a read session — no transfers are made.

How long does it take?

Usually under a minute. Multi-token wallets may require one extra confirmation per asset group.

Is my data stored?

Only aggregated compliance metadata, in line with our retention policy linked below.
